About this archive

Compiled and maintained by s0sta. Each entry documents a real loss event: the vulnerable contract's flaw, the attacker's flow with the actual numbers, and the transferable lesson for auditors and bug hunters.

Use it to learn attack classes, to calibrate severity, and as a pre-audit checklist: find the closest past incident and apply its lesson before you read the code.
